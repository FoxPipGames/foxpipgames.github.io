FoxPip ("we", "us") provides the mobile game "Triple Slide!" (the "Service"). We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information the Service processes, why, and the choices you have.

1. At a glance

  • There is no account or sign-up. We never ask you to type your name, email, phone number or address.
  • On first launch you see a screen to review the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy; you can read them again at any time in Settings.
  • The Service has no in-app purchases. We do not process payment information.
  • Your game progress (best score, achievements, settings) is stored only on your device and is not sent to our servers.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports are collected only if you agree. The game asks on first launch, the default is "off", and you can change your choice at any time in Settings.
  • The Service is free and shows ads (Google AdMob), which may use an advertising identifier.
  • Device information needed to show ads and to run the game engine (Unity) is processed whether or not you agree to the above (sections 2.2 to 2.4).
  • To show notices, the app downloads a notice file from GitHub Pages when it starts and when you open Notices in Settings, and your IP address and similar data are sent to GitHub. This also happens whether or not you agree to the above (section 2.7).
  • When the app starts, it asks Google Play whether a newer version is available; if there is one, you can keep playing after updating (section 2.8).

2. Information we process and why

2.1 Only if you agree: usage statistics and crash reports (Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics)

If you agree on first launch, or turn on "Send Usage Data" in Settings, the following is processed through Google Firebase:

  • If you do not agree, none of this is sent. The game works exactly the same.
  • This information is not linked to your name, contact details or any account.

2.2 Ads (Google AdMob)

To keep the game free, the Service shows Google AdMob ads. Google may process your advertising ID, device information, IP address and ad views/interactions to deliver ads and measure their performance. An interstitial ad may appear when you leave the results screen after a game (to play again or to go to the main menu). The Service has no rewarded ads.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and other regions that require it, the game first shows a short notice about ads, and then Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) shows an ad-consent form. If you do not consent to personalised ads, ads are still shown, but they are non-personalised (limited) ads. Players in those regions can reopen the choice from "Privacy Options" in Settings.
  • On your device you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or limit ad personalisation, under Settings > Privacy > Ads.

2.3 Personalised ads

The Google ads SDK in the Service processes information about your activity to show ads that match your interests. We do not collect or store this information ourselves.

The Service is intended for people aged 13 and over and does not knowingly show personalised ads to children under 13.

2.4 Information processed by the game engine (Unity)

The Service is built with Unity Technologies' game engine. When the app starts, the engine sends Unity anonymous statistics about the device hardware and operating system (device model, OS version, processor, graphics and memory specifications, screen resolution and similar) and the app and engine versions. Unity uses them to improve the engine's device support. They are sent whether or not you agree to usage statistics, and contain nothing that identifies you and no game records.

2.5 Information kept only on your device

Best score, the game in progress, achievements, sound/vibration/language and similar settings, your data-collection choice, your agreement to the Terms (the version and when) and the day you chose "Don't show again today" for notices are saved on your device. This is not sent to us. The app's data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so it is removed when you uninstall the app and is not restored if you install it again.

When you press Share on the results screen, the app makes a picture of your score in its temporary storage on your device and hands it to your device's share feature; it goes only to the app you choose. The picture and the share text are not sent to us, and the app you share to handles them under its own policy.

2.6 If you contact us

"Contact Us" in Settings opens your email app with the app version, device model and OS version pre-filled in the message. We receive your email address and message only if you send the email, and use them only to reply to you.

2.7 Service notices (GitHub Pages)

To show notices such as changes to the terms or this policy, and update messages, the Service downloads a notice file from our website (hosted on GitHub Pages) when the app starts (after you agree to the Terms) and when you open Notices in Settings. GitHub, which serves this file, may process your IP address, the time of the request and the type of device/app (User-Agent). We do not separately collect or view these access records. Because this is needed to run the Service, it happens whether or not you agree to usage statistics and crash reports, and nothing is requested while you are offline.

2.8 Update check (Google Play)

When the app starts, the Service uses Google Play's in-app update feature to check whether a newer version is available and, if so, shows Google Play's update screen. The check goes through the Google Play Store on your device, and Google may process the app's package name, the installed version and device information. We only learn whether an update is available and do not store it. Because this is needed to run the Service, it happens whether or not you agree to usage statistics and crash reports.

2.9 What we do not process

The Service does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location or photos, and does not process credit-card or bank details.

3. How long we keep information

  • Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics): event data is kept for 2 months and user-level data for 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports: kept by Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days and then deleted automatically.
  • Ad-related and update-check information: kept by Google under Google's policies.
  • Engine statistics: kept by Unity under Unity's policies.
  • Notice requests: kept by GitHub under GitHub's policies.
  • Support emails: deleted without delay once your request has been handled, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.
  • Information on your device: removed when you uninstall the app.

5. Your rights and how to use them

  • You may ask us at any time to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop processing it, and you may withdraw your consent.
  • Withdraw data-collection consent: turn off Send Usage Data in Settings. From then on nothing new is sent.
  • Ad consent, advertising ID and personalised ads: use the options in sections 2.2 and 2.3.
  • For information that Google, Unity or GitHub controls and we do not hold, you may need to use their own privacy tools; we will help you with that.
  • Send requests to the contact below. We will act without delay.

6. Deletion

When the purpose is fulfilled or the retention period ends, personal information is deleted in a way that cannot be recovered.

7. Security

We apply reasonable safeguards: least-privilege access to the accounts that handle information, encrypted transmission (HTTPS) and secure settings on administrative accounts. We do not run servers that store player information.

8. Children

The Service is intended for people aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we learn we have collected such information, we will delete it without delay. A parent or guardian can request deletion at the contact below.

9. External links

Links in Settings (the privacy policy, terms of service, and so on) open external websites, which are governed by their own policies.
